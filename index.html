About VS Plastic

Established in 2012 in Ahmedabad, VS Plastic is a reliable plastic injection moulding manufacturer. Under the leadership of Sanjay Patel, we specialise in high-quality curtain runners (Arabian Mindi Runner) and custom plastic components.

We handle shot weights from 1 gram to 150 grams using HDPE, PP, ABS, Nylon and PVC.
